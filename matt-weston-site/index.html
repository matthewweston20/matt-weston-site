About

I'm Content Operations Director at Invisible Media, a fast-growing digital media company covering economics, geopolitics, business and finance. In May 2026 we announced an equity investment from Goalhanger, the UK's biggest independent podcast group.

I've spent over a decade working for some of the world's most recognised publishers and creators. I've led social teams at Supercar Blondie (100M+ followers), Dexerto and LADbible Group, and I've managed 30+ professionals who have gone on to senior roles at The Diary of a CEO, MrBeast, High Performance Podcast and others.

I stay hands-on: Premiere Pro, Photoshop, AI workflow development. In an industry that changes faster than most, you have to keep up to give useful judgment.

Based in Manchester. Outside work: running, cricket, F1 and history.
